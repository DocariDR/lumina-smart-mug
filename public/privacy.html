Privacy policy

Lumina uses your email address to send the newsletter and launch updates you requested, and, for a pre-order, to confirm and manage your order.

Data collected

We store your email address, the date and version of your consent, and technical security data needed to protect the subscription service. Your IP address is stored only as a one-way pseudonym.

Pre-order data

When you place a pre-order, we store your full name, email address, delivery address, city, state, ZIP code, the quantity ordered, the amounts in USD and XOF, the exchange rate used and its date, and the date you accepted the terms of sale and this policy.

Orders are used only to process and deliver your pre-order and to answer questions about it. Payment is handled by the payment provider FedaPay: you are redirected to its secure payment page, where your payment details are entered and processed directly by FedaPay. Lumina does not store card data. Lumina stores the FedaPay transaction identifier to link your pre-order to its payment, and does not yet confirm payments automatically.

This is a demonstration: order data is kept only for testing and is deleted on request.

Cookies

The pre-order form sets one session cookie. It is strictly necessary for the security of the form: it protects against forged submissions. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Third parties

We use a third-party email delivery provider to send the newsletter on our behalf. It processes your email address only to deliver our messages.

Retention and rights

Your subscription remains active until you unsubscribe. You may request access, correction or deletion of your data by contacting the site operator. We respond within the applicable legal period.

Unsubscribe

Every newsletter includes a simple unsubscribe link. You can also use the link received by email at any time.
